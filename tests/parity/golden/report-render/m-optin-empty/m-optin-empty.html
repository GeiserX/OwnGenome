Mobile Element Insertions

Step 50 · Complete · link to this section

Alu, L1 and SVA copies the genome carries and the reference does not, found and genotyped by MEGAnE (Kojima et al. 2023) from short read pairs. Most are common and harmless; the catalog rows are insertions known to cause disease. Not callable: too few reads in that window to tell. Not seen is not proof of absence: short reads find an SVA's edges, not its body. Call filtered out: MEGAnE called an insertion there but flagged it (LC is low confidence), so it is not a finding, and not proof of absence either. Every call: mei/<sample>_mei.tsv.

Mosaic Loss of Chromosome Y or X

Step 33 · Complete · file date 2021-03-04 05:06 UTC · link to this section

Loss of chrY in a share of the blood cells of men, and of one chrX in women, becomes more common with age. Population studies link loss of Y with smoking and with higher rates of heart disease and some cancers; loss of X is less studied. That is a population-level association, not a diagnosis for one person. Below about 10% of the cells nothing can be told at 30x (docs/33-sample-qc.md).

Mosaic Chromosomal Alterations (MoChA)

Step 51 · Complete · link to this section

Large gains, losses and copy-neutral losses of heterozygosity (CN-LOH) carried by a share of the blood cells, from MoChA on the phased heterozygous sites of chr1 to chr22. In a UK Biobank study they became more common with age and went with a higher risk of a later blood cancer (Loh et al. 2018). That is a population-level association, not a diagnosis; an event is confirmed on a new blood sample. Below the floor nothing is reported, and an event above it can still be missed. Every MoChA call, with the reason it was or was not reported: mosaic/<sample>_mocha.tsv.

Locityper Second Opinion at Hard Loci

Step 53 · Complete · link to this section

A second opinion beside the primary caller, never the call itself. Locityper (Prodanov et al. 2025, Nat Genet, PMID 41107551) picks the two haplotypes of the HPRC pangenome panel that best explain the reads at each locus; it names haplotypes of panel people (<sample>.1 or .2), not alleles, so the two callers are shown side by side and not compared. Filter: the authors' HLA and KIR benchmark filter (no warning, weighted distance of 30 or less, fewer than 1000 or 20% unexplained reads). Every column: locityper/<sample>_locityper.tsv.
