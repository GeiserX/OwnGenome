Mosaic Chromosomal Alterations (MoChA)

Step 51 · Complete · file date 2021-03-04 05:06 UTC · link to this section

Large gains, losses and copy-neutral losses of heterozygosity (CN-LOH) carried by a share of the blood cells, from MoChA on the phased heterozygous sites of chr1 to chr22. In a UK Biobank study they became more common with age and went with a higher risk of a later blood cancer (Loh et al. 2018). That is a population-level association, not a diagnosis; an event is confirmed on a new blood sample. Below the floor nothing is reported, and an event above it can still be missed.
