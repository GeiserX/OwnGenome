Fine-grained ancestry of S (step 26b)
Reference panel: test; 37 of the 40 SNVs ADMIXTURE uses genotyped

Left out of the model: Oceania (continental and regional group), Southern Africa (regional group). The panel has fewer than 10 unrelated
reference people for it, too few to stand for a population, so the model has no such group and
any ancestry from it is spread over the groups below.

Continental groups (projection on the frequencies of 3 groups, 11 bootstrap replicates):
  West Eurasia (Europe, Middle East, North Africa)     90%  (80% to 90%)
  East Asia                                             5%  (5% to 5%)
  Africa                                                5%  (5% to 15%)

Regional groups (projection on the frequencies of 4 groups, 7 bootstrap replicates):
  Iberia                                               60%  (55% to 67%)
  Italy                                                39%  (32% to 44%)

Chromosome painting (og, continental groups) of chr2, chr20, chrX: share of the 546.0 Mb painted on the two haplotypes
  West Eurasia (Europe, Middle East, North Africa)     59%
  East Asia                                            18%
  Oceania                                              18%
  Africa                                                5%
  Not painted: the painting failed on chr3, chr19 (the step's log says why).

What this means: each name is a group of reference populations we put together and named,
not a nationality. The fractions say which groups your genome is most similar to; the range
after each is the 2.5 to 97.5 percentile of the bootstrap. Continental groups are well apart;
neighbouring regions are not: splits within Iberia are weak for every method, and Greek and
Southern Italian ancestry overlap (the panel has no Greek population at all). Read a regional
fraction with its range, never as one exact percentage. See docs/26b-ancestry-fine.md.
