To act on

Is there anything to take to a doctor?
Take to a doctor

Hereditary breast and ovarian cancer

BRCA2 · c.5946del · p.Ser1982ArgfsTer22 · one copy

One copy of BRCA2 carries a change that stops the protein early. People with such a change have a higher lifetime risk of breast and ovarian cancer, and to a smaller degree prostate and pancreatic cancer in male relatives. It is not a diagnosis of cancer.

What to do
  1. Bring this to a doctor or a genetic counsellor.
  2. Confirm it with a clinical test in a certified laboratory.
  3. Ask about screening and prevention; these start earlier than the general programme.
  4. Parents, brothers, sisters and children each have a 1 in 2 chance of carrying it. Elena's mother Carmen has no genome here.
Did Lía inherit it? The family analysis answers this. It is an adult-onset finding about a child, so it stays closed until you open it.

Worth a look

What deserves a conversation, but is not a diagnosis?
Worth a look

Fragile X repeat in the intermediate range

One copy of FMR1 has 48 CGG repeats: more than usual, below a premutation. It does not cause disease, but it can grow by a few repeats when passed on. Lía's copy has 49. Worth a word with a genetic counsellor before a future pregnancy.
