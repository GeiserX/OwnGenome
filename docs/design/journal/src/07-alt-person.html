Section 1 · Take to a doctor

One copy of BRCA2 stops short, and that raises the lifetime risk of breast and ovarian cancer.

One letter is missing from Elena's BRCA2, a gene that repairs broken DNA. From there the gene is read out of step, and the protein ends about 1,400 building blocks early, before the part that binds DNA.

It is not a diagnosis of cancer. It means screening and prevention start earlier than the general programme, and that her relatives each had a 1 in 2 chance of carrying it. Whether Lía did is closed on this page: it is an adult-onset result about a child.
