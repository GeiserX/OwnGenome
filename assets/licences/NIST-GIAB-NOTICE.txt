assets/callability_genes.tsv is built from Genome in a Bottle (GIAB) data of the
National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST): the CMRG v1.00 benchmark gene
coordinates and the GRCh38 genome stratifications v3.6 (segmental duplications, false
duplications, collapsed duplications). Changed: the genes those regions cover are
selected and each is tagged with its reason (scripts/ci/build-callability.sh); the date
of each change is the git commit date of assets/callability_genes.tsv. The notice NIST
